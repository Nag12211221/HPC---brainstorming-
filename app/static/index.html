Battery Thermal Management · POC for OEM management review

Why this subsystem?

Battery Thermal Management was selected for the POC because (1) modern EV packs already emit rich, real-time CAN telemetry (cell temps, coolant flow, current, voltage, SoC) that is easy to ingest, and (2) thermal-related claims drive the highest single warranty cost line in EV fleets — pack replacements run into five figures per vehicle. A 30–40% reduction in field claims pays for the platform many times over.
